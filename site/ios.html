Working, idle or waiting on you, at a glance.

WorkingIdleNeeds you

Every dot is an agent running on your Mac.

An animation of agents on a Mac: most are working or idle, one stops to ask a question and turns orange, and an answer from the phone sends it back to work.

The moment it's built for

When your agent stops to ask, your phone asks you.

Permission prompts and questions reach your phone the moment they appear.

  1. The agent stops. It needs a yes, a no or an answer.
  2. Your phone buzzes. The question arrives as a card, with the full command.
  3. You tap once. The agent carries on, with your note if you said no.
Agentman

The agent that watches them, too.

Ask what happened and answer its cards. The same conversation as your Mac.

Beyond the transcript

See what your agent changed.

The conversation tells you what happened. The workspace lets you look for yourself.

Files

Browse the workspace.

Open folders in the agent's working directory and find the file you want. Code and images open in a read-only viewer; private files and symlinks stay hidden.

Changes

Review the actual diff.

See staged, unstaged and new files since the last commit. Open a change to compare lines, with additions and removals clearly marked.

Read code and view images

Open source files with syntax highlighting and line numbers, or inspect an image right on your phone.

Open the live site

Open a server your agent started in the app. Share a preview link or stop the server from your phone.

Send images with your message

Attach a screenshot to show what you mean. Images your agent produces or inspects also open from the conversation.

From anywhere

Stay in charge of the work.

Every agent, not just one: the same controls for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Kiro and Antigravity.

Review the plan first

Plans and walkthroughs an agent writes open on your phone. Approve them, or send them back with a note.

Switch mode and model

Move an agent into plan mode or onto another model mid-session. If a switch would change your Mac's defaults, it asks first.

Say no, and say why

Refuse an action and the agent hears what to do instead, without leaving the question it asked.
