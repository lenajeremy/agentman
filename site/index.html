Why Agentman?

Agentman is an open-source, account-free remote control for coding agents, with live status, one-tap answers and a relay that stores nothing.

One dot per agent. Orange means it's waiting on you.

See it live

Every session on your Mac, grouped by what needs you first. Open one to follow along as it works.

Answer in one tap

Permission prompts and questions arrive as a notification and a card. Your answer goes straight back to the agent.

Send the next step

Message a running agent from your phone. It's typed into the terminal, or handed over when the turn ends.

The moment it's built for

When your agent stops to ask, your phone asks you.

Permission prompts and multiple-choice questions show up on your phone the moment they appear in the terminal.

  1. The agent blocks. Claude Code or Codex shows a prompt in its terminal, or OpenCode raises one through its API.
  2. Your phone buzzes. The question appears as a card, with the full command in view.
  3. You tap once. Your choice goes back to the agent and it carries on.

How it works

Three parts. Only your Mac holds the history.

# connect this Mac to the relay
$ am serve

# show a one-time code for your phone
$ am pair

# start an agent you can answer from anywhere
$ am claude

Your Mac

The daemon finds sessions, reads transcripts from disk and types into the terminal for you.

A stateless relay

Checks credentials and forwards live updates. Use the public one or run your own.

Your phone

Shows live state, loads history when you scroll back and sends your answers.

No account. Nothing stored in the middle.

Pair with a code that works once. History stays on your Mac. Push alerts carry a session name, not your code. Live traffic passes through the relay, so you can run your own with one Docker command.

Get started

Install the CLI, pair your phone, start an agent.
$brew install lenajeremy/agentman/agentman

Then run am install-hooks, am serve and am pair. Claude Code and Codex also need brew install tmux.

$go build -o bin/am ./cmd/am

Build from a clone with Go 1.26, or download a binary for amd64 or arm64 from the latest release.

The iPhone app is on its way to the App Store. Until review finishes, it's available as a TestFlight beta, and the invite link will be here shortly.

Build the app from source with Expo. It runs and shows alerts while it's open; background push isn't set up on Android yet. Mobile setup guide
